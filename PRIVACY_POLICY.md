# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 5, 2026

## Introduction

Swift Quote ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information Stored Locally

Swift Quote stores all your data locally on your device. This includes:

- **Quotation Data:** Customer names, addresses, phone numbers, and quotation details you enter
- **Business Information:** Company name, tagline, phone number, address, and logo you configure in settings
- **App Preferences:** Language settings, pricing preferences, and other configuration options

**Important:** This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

### Information Collected Automatically

When you use our app, we may collect certain information automatically:

- **Advertising Data:** We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data to serve personalized ads. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide and maintain the app functionality
- Store your quotations and business settings locally
- Display advertisements to support the free app

## Data Storage and Security

- We do not have access to your locally stored data
- We do not transmit your personal quotation data to external servers
