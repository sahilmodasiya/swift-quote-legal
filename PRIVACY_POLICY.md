# Privacy Policy

**Swift Quote**
**Publisher:** Dharma Services
**Last Updated:** October 16, 2026

## Introduction

Swift Quote ("we", "our", or "us") is an app for creating quotations and bills. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and how the advertising in the app works. Your quotation, bill and customer data stays on your device. We do not run a server that receives it.

## Summary

- Your quotations, bills, payments, customers and business details are stored on your device only.
- We have no accounts and no servers that receive your data.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads.
- The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
- When you share or export a quotation or bill, it goes to the app or location you choose.

## Information the App Uses

### Information stored on your device

- **Quotations and bills:** Customer names, addresses and phone numbers, item names, sizes, rates, discounts, taxes, charges, notes and totals that you enter.
- **Payments:** Payment amounts, dates and notes you record against bills.
- **Customers and products:** The customer list and the items you reuse.
- **Business information:** Your business name, tagline, phone number, address and logo.
- **Preferences:** Language, pricing preferences, numbering and other settings, and a daily share count.

This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us.

### Information collected automatically by advertising

The free version of the app shows advertisements using Google AdMob. When ads are loaded and shown, Google may collect and use:

- device identifiers such as the advertising ID
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- device and app information, and ad interaction and usage data

Google uses this to serve and measure ads, and may use it for personalized ads. Swift Quote does not give AdMob your quotations, customers, payments or business details. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can control advertising in your device settings: reset your advertising ID, delete it, or opt out of ads personalization (on Android, in Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google settings > Ads).

### Device permissions

Swift Quote requests the Internet permission, which it needs to load ads. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information in the app to:

- create, edit and store your quotations, bills and payments
- apply your business details, language and settings
- show advertisements that support the free app

We do not sell your information. We do not use your quotation and customer data for advertising or profiling.

## Sharing and Export

When you share or export a quotation or bill (as text, an image, a PDF, or a backup file), the app hands it to the app or location you choose, such as WhatsApp, email, your files or cloud storage. That content then belongs to that service and its privacy policy applies. Swift Quote does not send it anywhere by itself.

Backup files you export contain your customer and business data. Store them somewhere safe and delete copies you no longer need.

## Your Customers' Information

Quotations contain personal information about your customers, such as names, addresses and phone numbers. You decide what to enter and who to share it with. You are responsible for handling your customers' information lawfully and for keeping their details secure. We only provide the tool.

## Data Storage and Security

- Quotation, bill, payment and business data is stored locally on your device. We have no access to it.
- Protect your device with a screen lock. Anyone who can unlock your device can see your data in the app.
- On Android, your device's backup feature (such as Google's backup to your Google account) may include app data if it is turned on in your device settings. That backup is managed by your device and Google, not by us.
- No method of storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
- If you lose or replace your device without a backup, your data cannot be recovered by us.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google AdMob:** for showing advertisements. Governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Apps you share to:** such as WhatsApp or email, when you choose to share.

Swift Quote does not include analytics, social-media or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Children's Privacy

Swift Quote is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

## Your Choices and Rights

- Edit or delete any quotation, bill, payment or customer inside the app.
- Delete all data stored by the app by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
- Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings.
- Because we do not hold your data, we cannot access, correct, export or delete it for you. You control it on your device.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top. If we ever add accounts, cloud sync, analytics or any server that receives your data, we will update this policy before that change reaches you.
